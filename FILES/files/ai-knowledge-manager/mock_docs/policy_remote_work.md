# Policy: Remote & Hybrid Work
Document ID: POLICY-HR-021
Owner: People Operations
Last Updated: 2026-01-15
Status: Active

## Purpose
Establishes eligibility and expectations for remote and hybrid work
arrangements.

## Eligibility
- Employees who have completed 90 days of employment are eligible to request
  a hybrid arrangement (minimum 2 days/week in office).
- Fully remote arrangements require Director-level approval and are limited
  to roles tagged "remote-eligible" in the HR system.

## Expectations
1. Core collaboration hours are 10:00–16:00 in the employee's local time
   zone, during which employees must be reachable.
2. Remote employees must attend in-person all-hands meetings held quarterly,
   travel costs reimbursed per SOP-FIN-014.
3. Equipment (laptop, monitor, chair) is provided once per 3-year cycle;
   additional requests go through the IT Asset Request form.

## Review Cycle
Remote/hybrid arrangements are reviewed annually alongside performance
reviews and can be revoked with 30 days' notice if performance or
collaboration issues arise.
