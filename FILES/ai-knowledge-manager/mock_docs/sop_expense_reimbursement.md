# SOP: Expense Reimbursement Process
Document ID: SOP-FIN-014
Owner: Finance Operations
Last Updated: 2026-03-01
Status: Active

## Purpose
Defines the process for employees to submit and receive reimbursement for
business-related expenses.

## Scope
Applies to all full-time employees across Engineering, Sales, and Operations.

## Procedure
1. Employee submits an expense claim in the Expense Portal within 30 days of
   the expense date, attaching itemized receipts.
2. Claims under $150 are auto-approved if the category matches a pre-approved
   list (meals, local transport, office supplies).
3. Claims $150–$1,000 require manager approval within 5 business days.
4. Claims over $1,000 require both manager and Finance Director approval.
5. Approved claims are paid out in the next bi-weekly payroll cycle.
6. Rejected claims are returned to the employee with a written reason and can
   be resubmitted once with additional documentation.

## Exceptions
- Travel booked through the corporate travel portal is pre-approved and does
  not require a separate expense claim for airfare/hotel.
- Client entertainment expenses over $300 require a business justification
  note referencing the client account.
